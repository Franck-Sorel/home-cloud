## 1) Apply this bundle (hardening)

    helm install security oci://ghcr.io/Franck-Sorel/home-cloud-security

or locally: `just deploy security`

## 2) What it does

Creates default-deny NetworkPolicies (no ingress/egress by default) on the
namespaces you deployed, keeping every pod isolated until you explicitly allow
traffic. It leaves the essential kube-dns egress open so your apps can still
resolve names.

- `default-deny-ingress` — nothing can reach your pods unless you add rules
- `default-deny-egress` — pods can't dial out except DNS

Narrow the set with:

    --set namespaces={apps,storage,auth,secrets}

## 3) Compatibility

Safe to add last, on top of any bundle set. Layered with the `base` middleware
(security headers + rate-limit), authentication (`auth` bundle), Cloudflare WAF
(Terraform, edge side) = defence in depth.
