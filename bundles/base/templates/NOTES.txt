## 1) Apply this bundle (required core)

    helm install base oci://ghcr.io/Franck-Sorel/home-cloud-base \
      --set domain=mycloud.com --set mode=ddns

or locally: `just deploy base domain=mycloud.com mode=ddns`

## 2) Provide one credential Secret, depending on mode

- **mode=ddns** (default, $0): DDNS updater needs a Secret `ddns-credentials`:

      # deSEC:
      kubectl create secret generic ddns-credentials -n apps --from-literal=DESEC_TOKEN=YOUR_AUTH_TOKEN
      # Dynu:
      kubectl create secret generic ddns-credentials -n apps \
        --from-literal=DYNU_USER=you --from-literal=DYNU_PASSWORD=YOUR_PASS

  The updater keeps your public IP pointed at your hostname (deSEC/Dynu DynDNS2).

- **mode=cloudflare** (no open ports): the cloudflared pod needs a Secret
  `cloudflared-credentials` containing the tunnel credentials JSON:

      kubectl create secret -n cloudflared generic cloudflared-credentials \
        --from-file=credentials.json=~/.cloudflared/<tunnel-id>.json

## 3) HTTPS on your public subdomains

- **ddns**: Traefik's built-in ACME resolver gets the *wildcard*
  `*.mycloud.com` certificate. Wire it into the k3s Traefik chart using
  `bundles/base/traefik-values.yaml` (see comments in that file).
- **cloudflare**: TLS is terminated at the Cloudflare edge — nothing to do in-cluster.

## 4) Verify

    curl https://hello.mycloud.com     # "Welcome to home-cloud 🚀"
